Curious Minds Want to Know

Find answers to frequently asked questions about our interior design services, project timelines, and custom decor processes.

What is included in the initial consultation?

Answer. Our initial consultation is a deep dive into your space, style preferences, functional needs, and budget. We will walk through your home or commercial property, discuss your vision, and outline how our team can bring your design goals to life. 

How long does a typical design project take?

Answer. Timelines vary depending on the scope of the project. A single-room refresh might take 4 to 6 weeks, while a full home transformation or comprehensive renovation can take several months. We provide a detailed project schedule before any work begins. 

Do you source furniture and decor pieces for us?

Answer. Yes! We handle the entire procurement process. We source high-quality furniture, custom textiles, lighting, and unique decor items through our trusted network of suppliers and artisans, managing everything from ordering to final white-glove installation.
